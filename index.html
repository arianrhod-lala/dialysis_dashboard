03 / METHODOLOGY

Understand the limits.
Then explore.

A descriptive view of an existing aggregate export, not a validated prediction model.

What does the score represent?

The legacy formula is 25 × hypotension flag (mean SBP <100 mmHg) + 5 × cohort BP variability rank (1–4) + 10 × IDWG coefficient of variation (capped at 2). Maximum: 65. Bands are <15, 15–<30, and ≥30; these are experimental thresholds.

When is evidence insufficient?

The browser withholds scores for fewer than two sessions, missing or invalid variability or pressure values, nonpositive IDWG mean, and IDWG means below 0.5 kg where the legacy pipeline substituted zero. Aggregate counts cannot prove measurement completeness. Eligible scores retain exported values; no new clinical calculation is introduced.

What do groups and IDs mean?

Session groups and BP ranks come directly from the export's cohort quantiles. Session count has no time denominator and cannot establish treatment frequency or compliance. Age, sex, and dry weight form proxy IDs: people can be merged or split. Profile counts must not be interpreted as verified patient counts.

Reproducibility & provenance

The browser reads Patient_Dashboard_Data.csv. The existing Python pipeline requires the absent source workbook and pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and an Excel reader; it has unresolved identity, missing-data, and tied-cohort issues. Source redistribution permissions and deidentification need confirmation before publication. No generation timestamp or source-code repository URL has been supplied.
