A descriptive case study, with data preparation and uncertainty treated as part of the result.

From observations to a cohort summary

The original Python analysis grouped session observations and exported profile-level summaries. A separate build step now checks those summaries and produces only cohort counts for this website. It rejects blank or duplicate profile IDs. No profile records or individual-point plots are needed to present these results.

The exploratory score

The legacy formula is 25 × an indicator for mean systolic blood pressure below 100 mmHg + 5 × cohort blood pressure variability rank (1–4) + 10 × interdialytic weight gain coefficient of variation (capped at 2). The maximum is 65. Bands are below 15, 15 to below 30, and 30 or above. These hand-chosen weights and thresholds are not validated clinical predictions.

Why some scores are withheld

The build withholds numerical scores for fewer than two sessions, missing or invalid pressure or variability values, and mean interdialytic weight gain below 0.5 kg, where the legacy pipeline substituted zero variability. It also checks that the exported variation ratio and score agree with the documented calculations, allowing an absolute tolerance of 0.000000001 for serialization. Accepted scores retain their exported values. Measurement completeness cannot be established from these summaries alone.

What this study cannot establish

Age, sex, and dry weight formed the original proxy identities; different people may have been merged, and one person may have been split. Source measurement counts and follow-up time are unavailable. Recorded sessions cannot establish attendance or treatment frequency. There is no survival outcome or validated clinical model. Cohort summaries reduce detail in the website; they are not a formal guarantee of anonymization.

Reproducibility and next steps

The website loads a build-generated cohort summary. The original workbook is absent; the Python pipeline has unresolved identity, missing-measurement, and tied-cohort issues. Source redistribution permissions still need confirmation. The next research step is to establish stable source identity and per-measurement coverage before revisiting the score or attempting outcome modeling.
